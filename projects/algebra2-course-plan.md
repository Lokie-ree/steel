class the operator teaches — tracked here only so hub sessions know it exists.

## Role

Edmentum Algebra II, Semester A v8.0 — 6 units, 22 modules, 48 graded items, 0.5 credit.
Early College Option / IVLA, two sections (P-Tech Math, Instrumentation Math),
Aug 11 – Dec 18 2026. Applied content is anchored on the 4–20 mA current loop, one hook
per unit.

## Rules specific to this card

**Pacing and minute totals are derived, not transcribed.** They are asserted by
`verify_coverage.py` in the project, and computed answers are CAS-checked before
typesetting. Do not copy the pacing table into the vault; it will drift and the reader
will trust it. Same rule as git state ([[../CLAUDE]] §Git state is derived).

**Nothing about the run itself lands here.** Everything learned from teaching this with
students stays out of a public repo, in every form — that constraint is why this card is
a pointer and not a log.
